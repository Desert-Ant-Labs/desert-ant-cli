desertant 0.7.0 follows desert-ant-core 5.0.0.

- `da face photos/ --people` groups a photo library into people, each with the photos they are in. `da face photos/ --find selfie.jpg` lists the photos of the person in the selfie, most alike first, and `--strict` sets the match at 1 false match in 10,000 instead of 1 in 1,000. `da face photo.jpg` lists the faces in one photo. Face runs on macOS and Linux.
- Eye no longer needs `HF_TOKEN`: its weights download like every other model's.
- Eye runs on its v1.1.0 weights, and Moderator on v1.1.0.

Install or update with `da update`, `brew upgrade desertant`, or `mise upgrade`.
