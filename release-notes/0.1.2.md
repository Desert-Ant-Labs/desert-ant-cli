desertant 0.1.2 follows desert-ant-core 3.3.1.

- `da clips` and the other audio commands read recordings with many channels and files of several gigabytes.
- `da models` and `da info` show the 3.3.1 catalog: Align lists Linux and Windows, and Clear lists Core AI.

Install or update with `da update`, `brew upgrade desertant`, or `mise upgrade`.
