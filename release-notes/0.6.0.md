desertant 0.6.0 follows desert-ant-core 4.3.0.

- `da toxic "<text>"` checks a comment for hate, abuse, and threats before it posts, and names the group a hateful comment targets. Toxic reads 35 languages and is in beta.
- Flows have a `toxic` step.
- Uhm runs on Linux, and so does the `filler-words` flow. On Linux, Uhm finds each filler and its time; naming it uh, um, or hmm stays on macOS.
- A text model says when a text falls outside what it reads best: a language Toxic, Redact, or Emo does not list, a guess from Tongue, a word or two for Gist, or a long text for Emo. The note prints under the result on a terminal, and Tongue, Toxic, and Redact add it to their JSON as `notes`.
- A text that is empty or only spaces is now an error, as no text at all already was. Before, Gist tagged it with a topic and Toxic called it clean.
- Redact runs on its v0.4.2 weights, Voz on v0.5.0, and Who on v0.3.4. Who is faster on macOS.

Install or update with `da update`, `brew upgrade desertant`, or `mise upgrade`.
