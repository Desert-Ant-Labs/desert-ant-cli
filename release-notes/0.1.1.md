desertant 0.1.1 follows desert-ant-core 3.1.0.

- The loading label says a model is slower the first time only on its first load on a Mac, and again when its weights change.
- `da voz` reports how long the model took to load apart from the transcription, and the realtime factor covers transcription alone.
- Timestamps in `da voz`, `da uhm`, and `da clips` print in dark teal.

Install or update with `da update`, `brew upgrade desertant`, or `mise upgrade`.
